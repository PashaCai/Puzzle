# Face Puzzle — English remake

Open `Face-Puzzle-English.html` in Chrome or Edge. Portraits, handwriting font and the interactive 3D display are embedded; no installation is needed.

1. **Put yourself in the picture** — use your camera or upload a photo. Mark the image-left pupil, image-right pupil and chin to align its head size with the library.
2. Pick a donor portrait from the photo strip. Click any of the **2,080 tiles** to borrow that tiny area. **Show tiny pieces** reveals the grid. **Use as my base** replaces the whole starting face.
3. **Get into the details** opens the precision editor. Pick a donor, use a **1–100 px brush**, and zoom up to **800%**. The eye, nose and mouth buttons jump to the relevant region. Scroll to move around. **Undo stroke** removes the last stroke.
4. **Mix things up** borrows recognisable regions from several faces. Changing the base, a tile, or alignment clears brushwork, as stated in the editor.
5. **Turn it in 3D** opens the Colorfigures display. Drag to rotate, change the frame colour, or use Turntable. The mounted image includes your brushwork.
6. **Keep this face** saves a title, reflection and image to the local yearbook. Download a JPEG from each entry to keep an independent copy.

Photos and archives stay in this browser. If camera access is unavailable when opening the HTML directly, use photo upload, or run `start.cmd` from the source folder with Node.js installed. Real camera hardware was not tested during this revision.

Design: entirely English, Kalam handwriting, coral paper, ink outlines, yellow accents and a photographic cut-and-paste layout. Colorfigures supplies the rotatable display. Impeccable was not used for this remake.
